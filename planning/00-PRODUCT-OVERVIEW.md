# AI Agency Email Operating System — Product Overview

## What We're Building

A **multi-tenant AI Email Operations Platform** for agencies. Gmail is only the communication channel — the actual product is the **AI workflow engine behind Gmail**.

## Core Value Proposition

> AI-powered email operations for agencies that automatically captures leads, drafts replies, follows up, and keeps the CRM updated.

## Target Customers

1. **Your own agency** (Phase 1 — dogfood it)
2. **Other digital agencies** (web dev, marketing, SEO, automation agencies)
3. **Service businesses** (consultancies, law firms, real estate, financial services)

## Problem It Solves

Agencies drown in email. Leads get lost. Support takes too long. Follow-ups fall through cracks. CRM is never updated. This platform automates the entire email operations pipeline.

## Revenue Model

- **Subscription tiers** based on email volume (# of emails processed/month)
- **Per-seat pricing** for team members
- **White-label premium** for agencies to resell as their own
- **Integration marketplace** (future)

## Success Metrics

- Emails processed accurately
- Leads captured that would've been missed
- Response time reduction
- Human hours saved
- Follow-up completion rate
- CRM data accuracy

## Competitive Differentiation

- Built for agencies specifically (not generic email automation)
- Multi-tenant from day one
- AI + human-in-the-loop (not fully autonomous)
- Visual workflow builder
- Agency-specific knowledge base
- Can be white-labeled
