If you're building this for **your own agency first** and then selling the same system to other agencies, don't build a generic "AI Gmail automation." Build a **multi-tenant AI Email Operations Platform for agencies**.

The core idea:

> **Gmail → AI understands emails → classifies → extracts data → drafts/replies → triggers workflows → updates CRM → follows up → reports to team.**

You build the core once, then customize **prompts, workflows, integrations, and branding** per agency.

# 4. The most important architecture decision: Multi-tenancy

You want to sell this to multiple agencies.

Therefore, you need:

```text
Your SaaS
│
├── Agency A
│     ├── Users
│     ├── Gmail Accounts
│     ├── AI Settings
│     ├── Workflows
│     └── CRM
│
├── Agency B
│     ├── Users
│     ├── Gmail Accounts
│     ├── AI Settings
│     ├── Workflows
│     └── CRM
│
└── Agency C
      ├── Users
      ├── Gmail Accounts
      ├── AI Settings
      ├── Workflows
      └── CRM
```

Every database record should belong to an:

```text
organization_id
```

Example:

```text
Organization
    ↓
Users
    ↓
Gmail Accounts
    ↓
Emails
    ↓
AI Conversations
    ↓
Workflows
    ↓
CRM Contacts
```

This is critical.

If you build your own agency system first without multi-tenancy, then later try to convert it into SaaS, you will create unnecessary technical debt.

# 6. The actual AI email pipeline

When an email arrives:

```text
New Email
    ↓
Remove signatures
    ↓
Remove quoted replies
    ↓
Extract sender
    ↓
Extract subject
    ↓
Extract body
    ↓
Extract attachments
    ↓
AI Classification
```

The AI returns something like:

```json
{
  "intent": "new_lead",
  "priority": "high",
  "sentiment": "positive",
  "requires_reply": true,
  "lead_score": 87,
  "customer_name": "John Smith",
  "company": "ABC Inc",
  "phone": "+1...",
  "requested_service": "Website Development"
}
```

Then your workflow engine decides what to do.

```text
IF intent = new_lead
        ↓
Create CRM contact
        ↓
Assign sales person
        ↓
Generate reply
        ↓
Create follow-up sequence
        ↓
Notify sales team
```

This is the heart of the product.

# 13. The biggest mistake you should avoid

You said:

> "all of the Gmail automations for the agencies"

That's too broad.

If you try to automate everything, you'll build a bloated platform that doesn't solve one painful problem exceptionally well.

Your initial positioning should be:

> **"AI-powered email operations for agencies that automatically captures leads, drafts replies, follows up, and keeps the CRM updated."**

That's a clear business outcome.

Then you can expand.
